## A World Built on Real Biochemistry

Nothing in *Koku & Steam* is arbitrary scripting; the village runs on first-principles physiology:

- **Fuel Processing & The Randle Cycle:** Fatty acids ($\beta$-oxidation) and Glucose (Glycolysis) arrive on separate conveyor yards, competing for entry across the Membrane Bridge into the mitochondrial village. Excess glucose is stored in the **Glycogen Granary**, while fasting unlocks **Gluconeogenesis (GNG)** from Lactate, Glycerol, and—in extreme famine—Muscle Alanine ferried across the river from the **Muscle Outpost** while the mystical **River-Spirit Maiden** exports fresh glucose back through GLUT2.
- **Complexes I–IV & The Proton Dam:** Marked with subtle roof Roman numerals (`I`–`V`), Complexes I and II process fuel into CoQ flour sacks, Complex III rolls them into Cytochrome *c* mochi balls, and Complex IV bakes four at a time in its steam kiln with $\text{O}_2$—releasing metabolic water vapor while Complexes I, III, and IV pump protons up into the **Elevated Stone Dam ($\Delta\Psi_m$)**.
- **ATP, The Samurai Garrison & Adenylate Kinase:** Pressure from the Dam turns the Castle of Complex V waterwheel, minting golden **Koku (ATP)** for a conserved **Samurai Garrison** (`ATP + ADP + AMP`). Paid Samurai train in the Practice Yard until their Koku runs out, then rejoin the queue—or team up via **Adenylate Kinase** ($2\text{ADP} \to \text{ATP} + \text{AMP}$), pooling energy to intercept raiders while the donor rings the Wellhouse AMP alarm bell.
- **Redox Defense, Ketones & Uncoupling:** High dam pressure without demand stalls the wheels and sparks **ROS fires**, fought by the **GSH Wellhouse** fire brigade. Low-glucose, high-fat states awaken **Ketone Butterflies** ($\beta\text{-HB}$, AcAc, and exhaled Acetone)—with golden $\beta\text{-HB}$ dive-bombing inflammation fires, repairing ETC scarring, and calming prowling Ronin to extend endurance. Need to relieve dam pressure? Open the **UCP1 Spillway** to vent steam harmlessly into the **Onsen**.
- **AMPK Ninjas vs. mTOR Zen Monks:** Energy stress dispatches **AMPK Ninjas** for mitophagy, autophagy, and repair; anabolic surplus summons **mTOR Zen Monks** to build the Proteostasis Shrine.

## No Passive Shortcuts: Three Hard-Earned Win States

Just like real human physiology, **there is no lazy middle-slider setting that works forever.** Leave the village on autopilot and it will drift toward overload or depletion. Thriving in *Koku & Steam* rewards **active, intentional living**—with three distinct victory paths, each celebrated by a **Matsuri Festival** of glowing *chōchin* lanterns, drifting sakura petals, and fireflies (plus a **Continue Playing** mode so you can keep exploring your thriving cell):

1. **🏆 Super Capacity (`25 / 25` Samurai — Peak Mitochondrial Biogenesis):**
   Master the **3-phase physiological cycle**—push into **High Workload** ($\text{PGC-1}\alpha$ priming), pivot cleanly to **Recovery / Feeding** ($\text{mTORC1}$ supercompensation), and seal the gains in **Balanced Fasting** ($\text{AMPK}$ consolidation). Each completed cycle sends a Zen Monk into the Onsen to shed his robes and emerge as a new Samurai, expanding your garrison to peak `25/25` reserve.
2. **☯️ Metabolic Flexibility (`AMPK ↔ mTOR` Plasticity):**
   Actively oscillate between anabolic building and catabolic pruning—cycling your garrison up and down around `~20` Samurai (`16–24`) to prove sustained, dynamic cellular turnover.
3. **⚖️ Steady-State Homeostasis (`> 5` Minutes of Clean Balance):**
   Discover a rare, self-sustaining metabolic equilibrium—such as fasting down to a lean garrison and holding clean, active fat-adapted work for over five unbroken minutes with low ROS and zero crises.

*(Leave the village in prolonged sedentary fasting without exercise, and AMPK Ninjas will prune your garrison down to the **🪶 Austere Lean Minimum (`15/25`)**—a non-blocking advisory warning that spare surge reserve is gone, inviting you to exercise and refuel to rebuild.)*

## Six Real Pathologies — and the Power of Recovery

Push the village too far in any direction and real cellular pathology unfolds—each with its own unmistakable visual story, and each avoidable (or recoverable!) if you read the warning signs:

- **👹 Apoptosis (Overwork & Bioenergetic Collapse):** Starve the Castle queue under relentless demand and Samurai desert into menacing Ronin. Ignore the **Recovery / Feeding** window too long and `>60%` revolt—wiping out the village, blowing the Membrane Bridge ($\Delta\Psi_m \to 0\text{ mV}$), and committing *seppuku*, leaving only quiet *Toro Nagashi* lanterns drifting skyward.
- **☠️ Acute Necrosis & Ferroptosis (Toxin Siege):** Let Xenobiotic Bandits overwhelm GSH defenses and a bandit surge ravages the village, ignites every building, blows the bridge, and bursts open the Onsen sluice as they flee—leaving blackened carbon roofs and spreading ground fires threatening neighboring tissue.
- **🌾 Bioenergetic Starvation (Zero-Fuel Exhaustion):** Cut off both fat and glucose and watch the village burn through its last Lactate, Glycerol, and Alanine baskets while Ronin sheathe their swords and mill idly. Once every basket is empty, AMPK autophagy powers the village down into a silent, dull-grey brownout with rolling tumbleweeds.
- **🏛️ Cellular Senescence (High-Glucose Anabolic Overload):** Excess glucose alone drives unchecked mTORC1 construction until the towering Proteostasis Shrine reaches `100%`—drawing every villager, worker, and Samurai into a solemn circle around the temple amidst chronic SASP fires.
- **🫧 Hepatic Steatosis / NAFLD (Randle Collision):** Flood the sedentary village with **both** high fat and high glucose to trigger cytosolic yard inflammation, trapped golden lipid droplets, and insulin resistance—leaving Samurai with sheathed swords, workers moving in a sluggish crawl, and fires spreading across the island.
- **🧪 Warburg Fermentation — *With Press-Pulse Recovery*:** Severe ETC scarring (`>= 55%`) from toxins or unbuffered high-glucose ROS crumbles the Glycolysis Yard and Wellhouse into bubbling fermentation bogs. Even here, **recovery is possible**: apply the **Press** (`Glucose < 10%`, `Fat > 80%`, `Toxin < 5%`) and time **Pulse** strikes as Succinate overflows to heal the ETC back below `30%` and restore clean oxidative phosphorylation.
